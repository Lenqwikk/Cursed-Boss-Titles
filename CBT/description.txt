[h1]Ever thought Terraria bosses weren't cursed enough?[/h1]

[b]Cursed Boss Titles[/b] adds custom boss intro screens inspired by [i]Infernum Mode[/i] — but with much more questionable text. 

Every supported boss gets a custom title card with its own unique description, colors, and styling.
